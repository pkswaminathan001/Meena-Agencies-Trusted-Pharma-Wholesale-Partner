# Data Retention Policy (DPDP Act 2023)
- **User Data:** Retailer names, shop details, and PIN hashes are stored only as long as the account is active.
- **Order Data:** Order history is retained for 7 years as per tax regulations.
- **Consent:** Users must be shown a consent banner before any data is collected.
- **Breach Notification:** All data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours.
